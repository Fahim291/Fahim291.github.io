What I do

Monitoring only helps when readings are correct, arrive on time and show up where operators look. I work across all of those layers.

On site

Monitoring setup, sensor placement, preventive-maintenance and cleaning coordination, and working with plant operators day to day.

In the data

PLC tag and IP connectivity with plant PLC teams, Python telemetry uploads, ThingsBoard dashboards, and checking readings against expected plant signals.

When numbers disagree

I compare online sensor readings with laboratory results and separate calibration, sampling, method and data-quality causes instead of assuming one.

Where I’m heading

Industrial IoT, dependable data pipelines, and environmental monitoring technology.
